SecureWallet on USSD

Check your balance, send money and see your last transactions from any phone, with no data or smartphone needed. This simulator sends exactly what Africa's Talking sends, so the same menu works on a real short code once one is set up.

The USSD simulator is switched off. Add USSD_SIMULATOR=1 to .env and restart the server.

Dialling from your linked phone number…

Press Dial to open the menu as your own phone would.
What the server received

    Each reply starting with CON keeps the session open; END closes it. The text field carries every answer so far, joined by *. Sending money asks for your PIN and shows the recipient's registered name first. A first payment of KES 1,000 or more to someone also asks what it is for, as on the website.
