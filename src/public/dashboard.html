                    Account protection

                    Security

                    Your transaction PIN is a second check, separate from your password. When a payment looks unusual for your account, SecureWallet asks for the PIN before sending it, so someone who has got into your account without knowing the PIN cannot empty it.

                    Set your PIN

                    Avoid easy PINs such as 1234 or four of the same digit. Five wrong attempts lock the PIN for 15 minutes. If you forget your PIN it cannot be reset from here.

                    How payments are protected

                    • Low riskThe payment is sent straight away.
                    • UnusualYou confirm with your PIN, then it is sent.
                    • High riskThe money leaves your balance but is held. An analyst releases it or returns it to you.

                      What is checked

                      Each transfer, withdrawal and bank transfer is scored by the fraud model and compared with your own history: how often you are paying, how the amount compares with your usual payments, whether you have paid this person before, and whether the money is being passed quickly between accounts.
