How fraud checks work

Checked in milliseconds, reviewed by a person

  1. 01

    You make a transaction

    Transfers, withdrawals and bank transfers are recorded straight away, so your money is never held up waiting for a check.

  2. 02

    The model scores it

    A Random Forest trained on the PaySim mobile money dataset scores the transaction from its amount, type and the balances before and after.

  3. 03

    An analyst reviews alerts

    Anything scoring 0.50 or more goes to the fraud review queue, where an analyst confirms it as fraud or clears it. You see the outcome in your activity.

Save and borrow

Put money away, or get some when you need it

Lock money in a savings goal for 7 days to a year, or keep a flexible goal you can withdraw any time. When you are short, borrow up to 30% of what you received in the last 30 days, with a single 7.5% fee and 30 days to repay.

Accounts with a transaction confirmed as fraud cannot borrow for 90 days, so the fraud checks protect lending too.
