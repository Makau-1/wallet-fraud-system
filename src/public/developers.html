SecureWallet Fraud API

The risk engine behind SecureWallet, offered to other wallets, payment gateways and SACCOs. Send each outgoing payment before you complete it; act on the decision. A simulated service built for a final-year project: do not use it for real payments.

How it works

Each payment is scored by the machine learning model and checked against your own traffic for the patterns of account takeover and money mules: bursts of payments, unusual amounts, first payments to someone new, money passed straight on, new accounts collecting from many payers, and money going in circles. You get one of three decisions:

DecisionWhat to do
allowComplete the payment.
challengeAsk your customer for a second factor (a PIN or one-time code) first.
reviewHold the payment for a person to check.

Your customers' history is kept separately from every other client's; the engine judges them only against your traffic.

Authentication

Send your API key in the Authorization header. Keys are issued by the SecureWallet team and shown once.

Authorization: Bearer swk_live_…

Webhooks

When an assessment is challenge or review, your webhook URL receives an assessment.flagged event. Verify the SecureWallet-Signature header before trusting it:

SecureWallet-Signature: t=1763370000,v1=5f2b…

expected = HMAC_SHA256(webhook_secret, t + "." + raw_body)  (hex)
reject if expected != v1, or if t is more than 5 minutes old

Failed deliveries are retried after 1, 5, 15, 60 and 360 minutes. scripts/webhook-receiver.js in the repository is a complete receiver.
