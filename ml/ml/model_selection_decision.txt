SECUREWALLET MODEL-SELECTION DECISION
=====================================

Selected model: Random Forest Classifier

Alternative evaluated: Isolation Forest

Reason for selection:
Random Forest achieved an F1-score of 0.9820, precision of
0.9647 and recall of 1.0000 on the approximately 1% fraud
test set. On the unseen transaction-type experiment, it
achieved recall of 0.9602, compared with 0.0386 for Isolation
Forest trained on legitimate CASH_OUT transactions only.

Performance trade-off:
Random Forest had a measured scoring time of 73.0804 ms per
transaction. Isolation Forest A had a measured scoring time
of 39.0375 ms per transaction on the same computer.

Feature investigation:
The balance_discrepancy feature exactly matches
oldbalanceOrg - amount - newbalanceOrig in the processed data.
It has a Random Forest feature importance of 0.4623.
Removing it reduced the F1-score on the approximately 1% fraud
test set from 0.9820 to 0.6165.

Limitations:
The dataset is synthetic and its fraud patterns may be easier
to distinguish than real-world fraud. Results from the
approximately 1% test set come from a sampled subset of the
original test set. Performance in production is not established
by these experiments.

Final decision:
Use Random Forest as the candidate model for Sprint 4
integration, while preserving the experimental results and
validating feature calculations during integration.
